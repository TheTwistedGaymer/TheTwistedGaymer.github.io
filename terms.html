Permitted use

The software is intended solely as a personal desktop dashboard. No paid subscription, public account registration, hosted calendar service or commercial support is offered.

Google Calendar access

Connecting Google Calendar is optional. The app requests read-only authorisation and displays data from the calendars the owner can access. Google Calendar remains the authoritative source for event information. Cached data may be outdated when offline or when authorisation expires.

Availability

This preview software is provided without guarantees of continuous availability. The owner should verify important medical appointments, financial deadlines and other high-impact events directly in the originating calendar.
